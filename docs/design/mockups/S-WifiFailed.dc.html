Incorrect password

Your mirror couldn't join HarperHome

Common causes
  • The WiFi password was mistyped (passwords are case-sensitive).
  • The network is 5 GHz only. The mirror needs a 2.4 GHz network; many routers have a setting to turn 2.4 GHz on or split the bands.
  • It's a guest network or one with a sign-in page, which keeps devices from seeing each other.
  • The mirror is too far from the router.
  • Your phone isn't on HarperHome. Check Settings › Wi-Fi.
To try again

The mirror has reopened the SkyGlass-Setup network. Join it in Settings › Wi-Fi, then choose your network again.
