How it works
01
Walk up

The camera detects you and wakes the display in under 500ms. Face recognition loads your personalized dashboard.

02
Get briefed

Weather, TFRs, NOTAMs, and your schedule — all updated and waiting. No apps to open, no buttons to press.

03
Walk away

Leave, and it goes back to sleep. It's just a mirror again until you need it.

Know if you're flying before you finish your coffee.

Black stays invisible behind the glass. Every lit pixel earns its place, and colour only ever means something.

Live aviation weather

Real-time METAR and TAF with VFR, MVFR, IFR and LIFR colour coding, straight from aviationweather.gov.

TFRs & NOTAMs

Active restrictions and notices for your airports. Critical airspace info, automatically updated.

Your schedule

Google, Outlook, iCloud, CalDAV or a calendar link. Airline pilots can email in their XLSX schedule.

Presence detection

Wakes when you walk up, sleeps when you leave. On-device face recognition — no cloud.

Sun & moon

Sunrise, sunset, civil twilight and moon phase. Plan your night currency at a glance.

Gesture control

Wave to dismiss or sleep the display. No buttons, no touch, no voice commands.

Your layout, your call.

Drag, resize and hide widgets from the phone app and watch the mirror update live. Landscape or portrait, 24 widgets to choose from.
