Calendar link

Paste a calendar link to show its events. The mirror checks it for changes on its own.

Paste the full link, starting with webcal:// or https://
Google Calendar
On a computer, open Google Calendar settings, pick the calendar, and copy “Secret address in iCal format”.
iCloud
In the Calendar app, tap ⓘ next to the calendar, turn on Public Calendar, then Share Link.
Outlook
In Outlook on the web, go to Settings › Calendar › Shared calendars › Publish a calendar, and copy the ICS link.
Crew scheduling apps
Look for “Subscribe”, “iCal”, “webcal” or “Calendar feed” in the app’s export or sync settings.
Anyone with a private link can see that calendar, so keep it to yourself. It’s stored only on your mirror.
